Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Squibbi, developed by Elefterios Gabriel Ghidan (the “Developer,” “I,” “me,” or “my”), handles information in connection with the Game.

At a glance: Squibbi is a paid, offline game and does not collect, store, use, or share personal data.

1. Data Collection

The Game does not collect personal information, technical information, device identifiers, diagnostic information, or usage data.

The Game does not use cookies, advertising software development kits, analytics services, or third-party tracking technologies.

2. Accounts, Purchases, and Advertising

  • The Game does not contain in-app purchases.
  • The Game does not display third-party advertising.

3. Offline Functionality

The Game is designed to operate offline and does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

Your device or the platform through which you obtained the Game may independently connect to the internet for functions such as downloading, installation, updates, licensing, payment processing, backups, or platform security. Those activities are controlled by the relevant platform provider and not by the Game.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the Game does not collect personal data, the Developer does not sell, rent, disclose, or share personal data with third parties.

Information processed independently by an app store or platform provider, such as Apple App Store or Google Play, is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy and terms.

5. Data Retention and Security

The Developer does not retain personal data through the Game.

Because no personal data is collected or stored by the Game, there is no personal-data database associated with the Game that could be accessed without authorization.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not require registration and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or from any other users.

If you believe that information has been provided to the Developer outside the Game by a child, please use the contact information below.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal data. However, because the Game does not collect or retain personal data, the Developer generally has no Game-related personal data to access, correct, delete, or provide.

This does not affect any rights you may have in relation to information processed independently by an app store or platform provider.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, legal requirements, or clarification of existing practices.

The current version will remain available on this page and will be identified by the “Last Updated” date shown above.
